Built for high-value, fast-moving venues

Premium venues run on fast stock and high-pressure closes — exactly where margin quietly disappears. SAMePOS gives owners real-time visibility that used to arrive weeks too late.

Stop the shrinkage

Deterministic rules, statistical baselines and learned models flag voids, comps and stock variances as they happen — not at month-end.

Automate the busywork

Per-staff, per-tender cash-ups and supplier-invoice-to-stock processing are reconstructed automatically from your existing data.

Plug in, don't rip out

Run SAMePOS as a full point of sale, or as a read-only "sidecar" beside your existing system. Adopt the AI layer with zero disruption.

Two ways to run it

Full POS Mode

SAMePOS becomes your point of sale outright — fast bartender sales, tabs that follow guests, split payments, PIN-gated voids, live pricing and a roaming event dashboard.

Standalone Stock Sidecar

Keep your existing POS for sales. SAMePOS reads its nightly export and layers on stock control, variance analysis, cash-up automation and loss prevention — a low-risk wedge you can graduate from later.
